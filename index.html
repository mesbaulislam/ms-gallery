Help
Navigation
Arrow keys: move between photos
1 / 2 / 3: Photos / Albums / Favorites tabs
OK: open selected photo
Left key: Camera
Right key: Options
Back: go back / exit
Viewer
Left / Right: previous / next photo
1 / 3: zoom out / zoom in
Arrows (when zoomed): move around the photo
Editing
Options > Edit: crop, rotate, flip, brightness, contrast, filters and more
Folders
Albums tab: your photo folders (Camera, Download...)
Options > Trash: deleted photos - you can restore them
Options > Private: PIN-protected folder
Tips
Options > Slideshow: full-screen slideshow
Options > Search: find photos by name
Options > Settings: thumbnail size, sort order, theme, PIN lock
